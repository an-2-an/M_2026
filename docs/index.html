Faculty of Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical engineering is a key industry that ensures the development of the economy and technology!

We train specialists for enterprises in the machine-building, metallurgical and aviation industries. The mission of the Faculty of Mechanical Engineering is to train highly qualified specialists capable of solving complex technical tasks in the field of machine-building and metalworking. Our graduates have deep knowledge in the field of design, construction, production and operation of machines and mechanisms, they possess advanced technologies and are able to create the latest solutions for industry.
The year of establishment of the faculty is 1930.
